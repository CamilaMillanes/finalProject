Restaurant Name: Crumbl

Reviewer: Camila M.

Rating: 2.5/5

Review: This place doesn't really offer a dining experience. It's more of a grab and go place. As for the cookies themselves, some are very tasty like their chocolate chip cookie but some are overly sweet which makes it difficult to enjoy. Overall it's not worth the expesnive price.

Restaurant Name: Pizzeria Bianco

Reviewer: Kyndra P.

Rating: 5/5

Review: Super cute pizza place the pizza was great I got half the margarita and half sonny. I definitely recommend the Sonny over the margarita! Also got the flourless chocolate cake but it's very rich so I would ask for an extra side of the vanilla cream on top!
