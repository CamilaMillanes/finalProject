Featured Cultures

American culture

American culture is one that truly brings people together, especially in the United States. One specific food that brings a lot of people together are chocolate chip cookies!

Chocolate chip cookies are a true icon of comfort food and have a unique cultural story that reflects creativity, nostalgia, and global popularity. Their origin dates back to the 1930s, when Ruth Wakefield, the owner of the Toll House Inn in Massachusetts, stumbled upon the recipe. She intended to make chocolate cookies but decided to add chunks of semi-sweet chocolate to her dough, thinking they would melt evenly. Instead, the chocolate retained its shape, and the chocolate chip cookie was born—a happy accident that went on to revolutionize the baking world.

Italian culture

When people think of Italian culture they instantly think of pizza, but there are so many other dishes that are deeply rooted into italian culture. For example the caprese salad is one that many people know today.

The Caprese salad is deeply rooted in Italian culinary tradition, originating from the island of Capri in the Campania region of Southern Italy2. Its name reflects its birthplace, and the dish is a tribute to the vibrant colors of the Italian flag—red tomatoes, white mozzarella, and green basil. The Caprese salad is more than just a dish; it's a celebration of Italian culture, emphasizing the importance of natural flavors and the beauty of uncomplicated cooking.
